Three steps

No account creation, no card, nothing to configure.

1

Install

Add Loupe from the Chrome Web Store. Pin it to your toolbar so it's one click away.

2

Turn it on

Open any page and click the Loupe icon (or press Alt+Shift+K). The dock appears — press I and hover anything to start inspecting. Press P for the side panel with the full detail view.

3

Unlock Pro PRO

Invited by email? Open the side panel, hit any Pro feature (Figma compare, a11y audit, DS lint…) and choose Log in — enter the email your invite was sent to and everything unlocks instantly. No payment, no trial countdown.

Joining as a team?

If your team shared a design-system profile URL with you, open the panel's DS tab and paste it. The profile binds to your team's sites, powers token-aware inspection and lint, and — when it carries your org's licence — unlocks Pro for everyone automatically. No individual logins needed.
