What it does

Seven tools are free. The four marked Pro are a one-off $20.

Inspect I
Hover any element for its selector, box model, computed CSS, and ancestry. Screenshot it to PNG or copy its styles as a CSS rule.
Measure distances D
Click an anchor element, hover another — exact pixel distances between them, live, in DevTools style.
Nudge N
Move, resize and restyle elements on the live page: drag or arrow-key them with snapping to their neighbours’ edges, resize by handles, select several and move them as one, duplicate or remove a block to try the layout without it, edit padding, colour, type and radius from the panel, and rewrite text in place. A ghost of the original stays for comparison, ⌘Z undoes, and everything resets on reload — then export the change list for your developer or AI agent, with before/after screenshots.
Layout grid & rulers G R
Overlay a configurable column/gutter/margin grid, plus pixel rulers along the viewport edges.
Color picker C
A live magnifier samples the exact pixel under your cursor. Build a palette with hex/RGB/HSL, CSS-variable matches, and a WCAG contrast checker.
Xray & find X F
Outline the page's full element structure at a glance, or find elements by text or CSS selector and cycle through matches.
Responsive mode M
A centered device preview with 21 presets and zoom — or switch to in-place emulation with real mobile UA and touch for login and SSO flows.
Figma compare Pro
Anchor a Figma frame to your live DOM and get a per-element diff heatmap with severity grading and design-token hints.
Breakpoint batch compare Pro
Compare one Figma frame per breakpoint: Loupe reflows the page to each width and tabulates the differences.
Accessibility audit Pro
Page-wide scan for WCAG contrast failures, missing alt text, small tap targets, unlabeled fields, and heading-order issues.
AI fix suggestions & QA reports Pro
Optional AI-written fix suggestions using your own Anthropic API key, and one-click export of a standalone QA report.

Also in there: Tailwind xray S, design-system components O, DS lint L, and a searchable command palette.

How buying works

  1. Install Loupe — free from the Chrome Web Store. The whole inspector toolkit works right away, no account.
  2. Open a Pro feature — press Alt+Shift+K on any page, then P for the panel. The Design tab starts your free trial, no card.
  3. Upgrade when you're sold — one click in the panel, $20 once, yours forever. Your licence follows your email to any browser. Payments are handled by Stripe via ExtensionPay.

Team licences are a self-serve Stripe checkout — your signed licence file is issued the moment payment completes.

Privacy

Everything runs locally in your browser — no analytics, no tracking, no inspection data ever leaves your machine. Figma and Anthropic tokens are optional, stored on-device, and sent only to the API you connect them to. Full privacy policy.
